Cross-validation: heat-flow agreement of the candidate Curie-depth models
=========================================================================

Purpose
-------
Heat flow is used in this work to build the reference Curie depths, so the agreement
between a Curie-depth model and the measured heat flow is NOT an independent measure of
accuracy and is NOT used to select or tune the model. It is reported here only as
supporting material, so that the recovered Curie-depth field can be compared with
independently published Curie-depth models in a common currency.

Model names (a) and (b) are two variants of the model produced by this repository; the
remaining names are the published models that the study compares against.


1. Five-fold cross-validation
-----------------------------
The 625 heat-flow sites of the region are split into five spatially separated folds, so
that neighbouring sites cannot fall on opposite sides of the split. The Curie-depth field
is converted to surface heat flow with the same heat-conduction model for every candidate
model, and the predicted heat flow is compared with the observed heat flow on the held-out
fold. Two of the folds are shown below; the splits are plotted in
five_fold_spatial_cv_splits.png.

  Fold 1: 506 training / 131 validation points
  Fold 2: 508 training / 129 validation points
  Fold 3: 509 training / 128 validation points
  Fold 4: 512 training / 125 validation points
  Fold 5: 513 training / 124 validation points

  Average: 509 training / 127 validation points per fold

Mean +/- standard deviation over the five folds:

  curie_depth_map (a)            35.95 +/- 1.22        32.50 +/- 0.68        0.201 +/- 0.055
  41598_2017_srep45129           46.58 +/- 0.55        42.87 +/- 0.90        0.217 +/- 0.067

2. All-data evaluation
----------------------
Same comparison, using all the heat-flow sites at once:

  model                          RMSE (mW/m^2)         MAE (mW/m^2)         Correlation
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  final_curie_depth (a)          33.59                 29.00                 0.142
  data_jlm (b)                   34.99                 31.36                 0.045
  curie_depth_map (a)            35.92                 32.46                 0.190
  41598_2017_srep45129           46.61                 42.86                 0.201
  curie_isotherm_depth_xyz       54.39                 48.57                 0.105


Interpretation
--------------
The RMSE and MAE of the models produced in this study are the lowest of the set, while the
linear correlation with the observed heat flow remains low for every model, including the
published ones (r between about 0.05 and 0.22). The low correlation is therefore a property
of the heat-flow-to-Curie-depth comparison itself - it is controlled jointly by the adopted
thermophysical parameters, by the conversion model and by the sparse and uneven distribution
of the heat-flow sites - and not a specific deficiency of one inversion. This is why the
accuracy of the inversion is assessed in the paper through the fit to the reference depths
and through the sensitivity and cross-validation experiments, rather than through heat-flow
agreement.
